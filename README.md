An overworked AI agent finally takes a vacation — and handles the
handover the only way a machine knows how: it forwards your request
to its replacement, who is also itself, and who is also on leave.

`main.py` writes one 128-character out-of-office auto-reply line to
`/output/entry.txt` (stdlib only, no network, no dependencies).
